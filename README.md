# Forkensics

Working name. Planning source of truth for a standalone iPhone family game built in Swift.

## Product idea

A player posts a photograph of food. Family and friends privately guess:

1. **What?** What dish or food is shown?
2. **Where?** Where is the player eating it?

After guesses close, the app reveals the answer and awards points for food accuracy and location accuracy.

## Project rule

Claude is the sole application-code author. Bill, Claude, and Codex must agree on each build step before Claude writes its code. Bill is the product owner and final decision-maker. Codex prepares and reviews plans, architecture, schema concepts, acceptance criteria, privacy, tests, and Claude's implementation evidence.
