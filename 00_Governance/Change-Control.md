# Change Control

Every requested change is classified as one of the following:

- **Clarification:** does not change approved behavior.
- **Correction:** makes implementation conform to approved behavior.
- **Scope change:** adds, removes, or materially changes behavior.
- **New idea:** belongs in the backlog until prioritized.

Clarifications and corrections may proceed within an approved step after they are documented. Scope changes require a revised review packet and fresh approval. New ideas do not interrupt the current step unless Bill explicitly reprioritizes them.
