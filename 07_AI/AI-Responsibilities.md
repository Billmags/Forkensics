# AI Responsibilities — Draft

## Appropriate uses

- Suggest a possible dish to help the poster enter the canonical answer.
- Compare a player's free-text food guess with the poster-confirmed answer.
- Identify synonyms, spelling variants, and reasonable specificity differences.
- Generate optional hints after the poster approves them.
- Explain why partial credit was proposed.

## Inappropriate uses

- Secretly establish the official answer without poster confirmation.
- Reveal or infer the hidden location for players.
- Make safety, allergy, edibility, or health claims.
- Decide irreversible scoring without an appeal or deterministic fallback.
- Train on private family photos without explicit permission.

## Required safeguards

- Store the rule and prompt version used for judging.
- Prefer structured outputs and bounded score categories.
- Keep a deterministic no-AI path.
- Allow the poster or group administrator to resolve disputed food matches.
- Do not send location or identity information unless required and approved.
