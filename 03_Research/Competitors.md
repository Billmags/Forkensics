# Competitor and Alternative Notes

Initial research found adjacent products, but no exact match for the proposed game.

## Location-photo games

- Whereez — friends share photos and guess locations.
- PinMeDown — private photo posts with map-pin guesses and distance scoring.
- CloseUp — guesses faces, places, and dates from a personal camera roll.

## Personal-photo party games

- PicPop — players guess which friend submitted a photo.
- GuessGram and similar games — players upload photos and guess ownership.

## Food-geography games

- DishGuessr — identify the country associated with a supplied dish.
- FoodMapper — map-based guessing for supplied food images.

## Identified gap

Private recurring groups using their own current food photographs, with separate `What?` and `Where?` answers, delayed reveal, fuzzy food judging, geographic scoring, and persistent family standings.

Research must be refreshed before commercial positioning or App Store submission.
