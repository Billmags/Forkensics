# Roadmap — Approval-Gated

No phase authorizes code by itself. Each implementation step requires its own approved review packet.

## Phase 0 — Governance and discovery

- Confirm roles and approval process.
- Define the family game as it is played today.
- Resolve MVP rules and terminology.
- Approve product brief and success criteria.

## Phase 1 — Experience specification

- Approve core user flow.
- Approve screen inventory and wireframes.
- Approve challenge lifecycle and reveal rules.
- Approve scoring and hint behavior.
- Approve accessibility expectations.

## Phase 2 — Technical specification

- Approve iOS target and Swift architecture.
- Choose authentication, backend, database, and media storage.
- Approve conceptual schema and ownership rules.
- Approve notifications, offline behavior, and synchronization.
- Approve privacy, security, deletion, and abuse protections.
- Approve AI scope and cost controls.

## Phase 3 — Claude implementation steps

Potential step order, subject to review:

1. App shell and local navigation
2. Local challenge creation prototype
3. Local guessing and reveal prototype
4. Authentication and private groups
5. Server-backed challenges and media
6. Scoring and standings
7. Notifications and asynchronous lifecycle
8. AI-assisted answer comparison
9. Privacy, deletion, accessibility, and resilience hardening
10. TestFlight readiness

## Phase 4 — Family pilot

- Test with the real family group.
- Measure posting time, guess completion, disputes, and drop-off.
- Fix rules before adding feature breadth.

## Phase 5 — Broader release consideration

- Naming and trademark review
- App Store requirements
- Privacy policy and terms
- Support and moderation plan
- Pricing decision
