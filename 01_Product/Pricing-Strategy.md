# Forkensics — Free Tier + Monetization Strategy

**Status:** Launch hypothesis — adjust based on real usage, conversion, retention, and posting frequency post-launch.

---

## Core Principle

Posting cases is naturally scarce. Guessing is naturally abundant.

Most Forkensics users will probably only have a few restaurant/food photos worth turning into cases each month, while they could easily make dozens of guesses. Because of that, giving users one free case post per day would effectively mean unlimited posting for most users and remove a major reason to upgrade.

---

## Free Investigator

Forkensics should remain completely free to download so there is no barrier when someone discovers a case through Facebook, Reddit, a friend, or another shared link.

### Free Case Creation

Give each new user **3 free case posts total** — lifetime starter cases, not three per day or per month. Three cases should be enough for someone to experience the creator side of Forkensics and see people interacting with their cases. After using their three free cases, additional case creation requires Forkensics Unlimited.

### Free Guessing

Guessing should be considerably more generous because free guessers make the entire Forkensics ecosystem more valuable.

Starting structure:
- **5 free investigations/guesses per day**
- Once the daily allowance is used: watch one rewarded ad → unlock additional guesses
- The exact number of guesses earned per ad can be tested after launch

Free players should never feel useless. They provide guesses, competition, engagement, and activity for the people posting cases.

---

## Rewarded Ads

Do not show an ad before every guess. That would interrupt the core gameplay loop and make Forkensics feel like a cheap mobile game.

Instead, rewarded ads should be optional: "Want to keep investigating? Watch a short ad to unlock 5 more cases." The player chooses the ad because they want to continue playing.

Rewarded ads should primarily extend guessing, not case creation. Do not allow users to endlessly watch ads to create new cases — case creation is one of the primary benefits of becoming a paying Forkensics member.

---

## Forkensics Unlimited

| Plan | Price |
|------|-------|
| Monthly | $1.99/month |
| Annual — Best Value | $9.99/year |

The $9.99/year option should be the primary/hero offer.

Unlimited includes:
- Unlimited case creation
- Unlimited investigations/guesses
- No ads
- Full stats
- Complete case history
- Expanded leaderboard features
- Additional clues/gameplay features
- Future premium features

---

## Why This Structure Works

The goal is not simply to determine whether someone "can afford $10." The goal is to let someone experience enough of Forkensics that $9.99/year feels obviously worth it.

A typical conversion journey:

> See shared food case → Download Forkensics free → Guess → Browse more cases → Keep playing → Remember a great restaurant photo → Create first case → See people guessing → Create another → Create third case → Want to post again → **Upgrade to Unlimited**

At that point, the user has already demonstrated that they enjoy the product. The annual price is approximately **$0.83/month**, which should feel very inexpensive to an engaged Forkensics player.

---

## Strategic Rule

**Protect guessing. Monetize posting and heavy usage.**

Forkensics needs a large population of people making guesses. Guessers create the engagement that makes posting a case fun.

- A free player who never pays but regularly guesses and occasionally watches rewarded ads is still valuable to Forkensics.
- A highly engaged player who wants to post cases regularly, play without limits, remove ads, and access their full history/stats becomes the ideal Forkensics Unlimited customer.

### FORKENSICS UNLIMITED
