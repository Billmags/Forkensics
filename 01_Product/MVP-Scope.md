# MVP Scope — Draft for Review

This is a discussion draft, not approved build scope.

## Candidate MVP capabilities

- Sign in and maintain a simple player profile.
- Create or join an invite-only group.
- Capture or select one food photograph.
- Confirm the correct dish, venue, city, and map location.
- Add an optional comment or story.
- Publish a challenge without exposing source-photo metadata.
- Notify group members that a round is open.
- Submit one `What?` guess and one `Where?` guess.
- Edit a guess until the deadline or first lock action.
- Prevent players from seeing other guesses before reveal.
- Automatically or manually close and reveal a round.
- Award transparent food and location points.
- Display round results and a group leaderboard.
- React or comment after reveal.
- Report, delete, or leave a group.

## Explicitly deferred candidates

- Public discovery feed
- Advertising
- Android app
- Live video
- Restaurant reservations or reviews
- Automatic public social posting
- Cash prizes or gambling mechanics
- AI-generated official answers without poster confirmation
- Complex achievements, leagues, or tournaments
- Broad travel-journal features
