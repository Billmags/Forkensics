# Product Brief — Forkensics

## Vision

Turn a family tradition into an easy private game: send a food picture, then let family and friends guess what the person is eating and where they are eating it.

## Core promise

**See the plate. Guess the food. Pin the place.**

## Audience

Families and close friend groups who enjoy food, restaurants, travel, friendly competition, and lightweight asynchronous games.

## Core loop

1. A player creates a private challenge using a new or existing food photo.
2. The poster supplies and confirms the correct dish and location.
3. The app publishes a privacy-safe copy of the image to an invited group.
4. Other players guess `What?` and `Where?` before the deadline.
5. Guesses remain hidden until reveal.
6. The app reveals the dish, venue/location, story, and scores.
7. Players react, discuss, and see updated standings.

## Product principles

- Private groups, not a public social network.
- The poster confirms the truth; AI does not invent the official answer.
- Fast enough to post or guess in under a minute.
- Fun even when a player is wrong.
- Exact location remains concealed until reveal.
- Rules are understandable without a tutorial.
- The first release serves the existing family game before adding broader modes.

## Differentiation

Nearby products cover personal photo games, location guessing, or food-geography quizzes. The distinctive combination is private personal food photos plus separate dish and real-location guesses, delayed reveal, and persistent family standings.

## Success definition

A family group can complete recurring rounds without returning to text-message bookkeeping, while preserving the teasing, conversation, and shared discovery that make the original game enjoyable.
