# Initial Claude Handoff

What & Where? is a standalone iPhone app written in Swift. It converts Bill's existing family game into a private asynchronous experience: one player posts a food photograph, and others guess both what the food is and where the player is eating it.

The three reviewers are Bill, Claude, and Codex/GPT. Bill is product owner and final decision-maker. Claude is the sole application-code author. Codex prepares and reviews product plans, architecture, conceptual schema, privacy, acceptance criteria, test strategy, and Claude's implementation evidence. Codex will not write application code.

No code may be written until all three have reviewed the same step proposal and Bill explicitly approves it. Claude should challenge feasibility and propose changes before approval, then implement only the approved scope. After implementation, Claude supplies changed files, tests and results, manual verification, limitations, and scope confirmation. Codex reviews that evidence before Bill accepts the step.
